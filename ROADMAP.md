## Later

| Idea | Why it isn't in yet |
|---|---|
| "Play while downloading" button that opens the player | It has sequential download; it never opens files by itself, so playing is up to you. |
| Port forwarding through a VPN that offers it | NordVPN doesn't; UPnP on the home router would bypass the VPN, so it stays off. |
| Bind local peer discovery to the VPN | LPD is multicast on the home network; it's off by default instead. |
| v2 / hybrid torrents in the creator | 1.1 makes v1 torrents (what every client and tracker understands). |
